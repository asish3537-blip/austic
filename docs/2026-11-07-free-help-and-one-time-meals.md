# Free help, WhatsApp support and one-time meals — 7 November 2026

## Using voice Help

1. Open **Paustik help** in the website or app.
2. Tap **Talk to Paustik**. Allow microphone access in your browser if requested.
3. Ask an English question about Paustik. Recording stops after one question or after 20 seconds. Stop listening, closing Help, changing account or hiding the page ends capture.
4. Review the transcription, then press **Send**. Speech never automatically places an order, changes an account, or submits a message.
5. Tap **Read reply aloud** to hear the latest reply. Stop reading ends playback.

This feature uses browser speech recognition/synthesis plus Paustik's existing guided answers. The widget explicitly requests guided-only responses, bypassing paid AI calls even if another AI feature is configured. No new subscription, API key, database or paid service is required. This is guided app help, not unrestricted generative AI. English voice support varies by device/browser, especially Android WebViews; typing stays available. Physical microphone recognition and audible speaker output require a check on the actual user's device. Browsers may send speech to their own online recognition service; Paustik does not upload or retain raw audio. The reviewed text is sent to Paustik Help only after Send is pressed.
